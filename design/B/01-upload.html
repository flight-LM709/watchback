Liner notes · 6 steps

Get your file from Google Takeout

01Go to takeout.google.com and tap Deselect all.
02Scroll to YouTube and YouTube Music and tick it.
03Tap All YouTube data included and leave only history ticked. It makes the file much smaller.
04Tap Multiple formats and set History to JSON. HTML works too, but JSON is faster.
05Tap Next step, choose Export once and .zip, then Create export.
06Google emails you when it’s ready, which can take a few minutes or a few hours. Download the .zip and drop it here. No need to unzip it.
