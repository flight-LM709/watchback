# Watchback design mockups

Two candidate visual directions. vedrico hasn't picked one yet. All data is fake example data.

- `A-contact.png`, `A/`: **Neon Rewind**. Dark backgrounds, a gradient pair per slide, Space Grotesk.
- `B-contact.png`, `B/`: **Paper Mixtape**. Cream paper, a collage feel, Fraunces + Space Mono. The B HTML is generated by `B/gen_b.py`.

Screens: 01 upload, 02 big number, 03 top creator, 04 prime time, 05 share card (390x844 @2x).
Re-render with `npm i && node render.js A B` (needs Chrome).

## Rules that apply to both directions
- Story slides are 9:16. Tap right or left to move, hold to pause, with progress bars at the top.
- One hero number per slide, at least 96px on mobile. Headlines are 28-32px. At most one chart per slide.
- Every number has a unit. Watch time always shows "≈" and an "estimate" chip that opens the explainer.
- Video titles clamp to 2 lines and creator names to 1 line, using CSS `line-clamp`.
- The period pill sits at the top of every stat slide. It shows "{Mon YYYY} – {Mon YYYY}", "{year}" or "All time".
- One time-of-day badge per person, shown on the prime-time slide as `{badge}, {pct}% of plays`.
- Watch time unavailable: drop slide 5, and on the share card the watch-time tile becomes "Your peak hour".
- No YouTube logo or red play button. "Not affiliated with YouTube or Google." goes on the upload screen and the share card.
- Text meets WCAG AA contrast, and `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.
