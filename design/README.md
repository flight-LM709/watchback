# Watchback design: Paper Mixtape

**vedrico chose Direction B, Paper Mixtape** (cream paper, collage, cassette/VHS details, Fraunces + Space Mono, with Caveat for hand-written notes). Direction A (Neon Rewind) was dropped from this branch and is archived in `/workspace/rewind-mockups`. All data in the mockups is fake example data.

- `SPEC.md`: the build spec for Frontend Dev (components, slide order, motion, hit areas, clamping, fallbacks, export sizes).
- `tokens.css`: Tailwind 4 `@theme` + plain CSS variables (colours with WCAG ratios, fonts, type scale, spacing, radii, shadows, paper grain).
- `assets/`: reusable SVGs (hand circle, underline, arrow, star, sparkle, tape, cassette outline, cassette icon, VHS label frame, check/x scribbles, grain).
- `B/`: mockups. The HTML is generated by `B/gen_b.py`, and the story order is in `B/order.json`. They render at 390×844 @2x, except `13-share-square` (1080×1080).
- `B-contact-full.png`: every screen in story order.

Re-render: `npm i && (cd B && python3 gen_b.py) && node render.js B` (needs Chrome at `/usr/bin/google-chrome`).

## Rules
- Story slides are 9:16. Tap right or left to move, hold to pause, with progress bars at the top.
- One hero number per slide, at least 96px on mobile. Headlines are 28-32px. At most one chart per slide.
- Every number has a unit. Watch time always shows "≈" and an "Estimate" chip that opens the explainer.
- Video titles clamp to 2 lines. Creator, artist, song and search strings clamp to 1 line.
- The period pill sits at the top of every stat slide. It shows "{Mon YYYY} – {Mon YYYY}", "{year}" or "All time", and opens a bottom sheet.
- One time-of-day badge per person, shown on the prime-time slide as `{badge}, {pct}% of plays`.
- Watch time unavailable: drop the watch-time slide, and on the share card the watch-time tile becomes "Your peak hour". No Music data: skip the music total and top songs slides. No Shorts detected (or no long‑form): skip both Shorts slides (16, 17).
- No YouTube logo or red play button. "Not affiliated with YouTube or Google." goes on landing, upload and the share cards.
- Text meets WCAG AA contrast (pairs listed in `tokens.css`), and `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.
- All strings come from `src/copy/en.ts`.
